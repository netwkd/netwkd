NW0014. Physics steps do not divide evenly into ticks
=====================================================

Reported as a warning when the physics rate is not a whole multiple of the
tickrate.

Peers drift apart because a tick then covers a different number of physics
steps from one tick to the next. The log line gives both rates.

.. code-block:: gdscript

    func _init() -> void:
        Netw.configure_clock(self).ticks_per_second(45)

With physics at 60 steps per second, 45 ticks do not fit. Set
``physics_ticks_per_second`` under Physics, Common in the project settings to
a whole multiple of
:ref:`NetwClockConfig.tickrate <class_NetwClockConfig_property_tickrate>`,
or change the tickrate to match.

.. code-block:: gdscript

    func _init() -> void:
        Netw.configure_clock(self).ticks_per_second(30)
