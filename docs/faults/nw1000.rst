NW1000. A link to a peer or service failed
==========================================

Reported as a warning when a transport, tracker, signing key or peer cannot
be reached or refuses a packet.

What the call set out to do did not happen. The log line names the peer or
service and the step that failed.

Check the network and the transport, and connect again.

.. code-block:: gdscript

    func _on_retry_pressed() -> void:
        connection.join(address)
