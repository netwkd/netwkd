NW1021. A route did not become live in time
===========================================

Reported as a warning when a packet names an entity that has not spawned on
this peer, and the entity does not spawn before the packet's wait times out.

The packet was dropped. The log line gives the route.

Make sure the server sends the entity at that route to this peer.
