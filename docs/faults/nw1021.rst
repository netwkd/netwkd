NW1021. A route did not become live in time
===========================================

Reported as a warning when a route is not live by the timeout given to
:ref:`NetwMultiplayer.liveness_when_live <class_NetwMultiplayer_method_liveness_when_live>`.

The callback was dropped. The log line gives the route.

Pass a longer timeout, or wait only on a route this peer will receive.
