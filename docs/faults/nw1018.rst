NW1018. A limit was exceeded
============================

Reported as a warning when something needs more than a fixed limit allows,
such as the player list or a spawn.

What went over the limit was dropped. The log line gives the need, the limit
and what was dropped.

Stay under the limit. The log line names the setting, such as a higher
``spawn_limit``, or the change, such as fewer spawned nodes or more scenes for
the players.
